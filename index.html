My Journey

Education & Career — the story so far

  • 2018

    Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham

    Began my undergraduate journey in Computer Science & Engineering. The start of countless late-night coding sessions, caffeine-fueled discoveries, and a lifelong love affair with debugging.

  • 2020

    Building & Breaking Things

    Dove deep into Machine Learning, Web Development, and Bot Development. Built Discord bots, scraped the internet, trained models — and learned that git push --force has consequences.

  • 2022 – Present

    Zscaler — Securing the Cloud

    Graduated from Amrita and joined Zscaler — one of the global leaders in cloud security. Working on Out-of-Band CASB, SSPM & Posture Management across enterprise SaaS platforms.

    • 2022 – 2023

      Associate Software Engineer

      Fresh out of college, armed with a degree and dangerous amounts of enthusiasm. Learned that production code is nothing like college assignments.

    • 2023 – 2024

      Software Engineer

      Started owning features end-to-end. Built API integrations, tamed Kafka consumers, and discovered that "it works on my machine" is not a valid deployment strategy.

    • 2024 – Present

      Senior Software Engineer

      Leading architecture decisions, mentoring engineers, and scaling systems to handle millions of events. The bugs fear me now. Mostly.

  • 2025

    Google AI Hack ’25 Champions & Patent

    Won Google AI Hack’25 Champions for building a Data-Backed DLP Policy Recommendation Engine. The project was taken internally as a patent and submitted to the USPTO. From hackathon prototype to intellectual property — not a bad year.
